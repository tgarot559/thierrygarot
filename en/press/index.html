Short bio

Thierry Garot has been building projects for more than 25 years and is a speaker. Born in Abidjan and based on France’s Atlantic coast, he is behind Interface, a scheme launched in Niort in the early 2000s that gave business founders a guarantee before they applied for a bank loan. He then built bank partnerships at Unilend and Meilleurtaux.com. He founded Votre Zone de Génie, getLinora, Sillaj and VerifAmende, and raises awareness of accessibility in companies with Inclusio Formation. The father of four children adopted in Thailand, he is listed in the Top 100 Handicap France. Along the way, he humbly discovered his zone of genius and his life's mission.
